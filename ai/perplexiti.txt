A high-converting real estate plots website should revolve around your two active ventures and strong lead capture on every key page. Below is a structured, agency-grade sitemap and feature list tailored for a residential plot-focused business with secondary services like buying/selling support, Building Mantenance, and construction.

Essential sections on key pages
Home page

    Hero section:

        Strong headline focused on residential plots and trust.

        Subheadline explaining main value proposition and geography.

        Primary CTA: “View Our Plot Ventures” + secondary CTA: “Schedule a Site Visit”.

    ​

Highlight of active ventures:

    Cards for the two primary ventures with brief USPs, location, price range, and CTA to view details.

    ​

Quick property search:

    Filters for location, plot size, budget, venture name, and purpose (investment / self-use).

    ​

Trust & credibility:

    Logos of approvals (DTCP/Governament etc.), bank tie-ups, partner brands.

    Short stats (years in business, number of plots sold, families served).

    ​

Featured benefits:

    Bulleted reasons to choose this agency (clear titles, gated layouts, amenities, transparent process).

Testimonials snapshot:

    2–3 client quotes with photos, link to full testimonials page.

        ​

    Lead capture strip:

        Short form: Name, Phone, Preferred Location, Budget + consent checkbox.

Ongoing venture detail page (for each plot project)

    Project summary:

        Name, tagline, Governament/approval details, location overview, starting price, status (launching / booking open).

    ​

Gallery:

    High-quality images, layout photos, amenity visuals, drone shots, and a short video/virtual tour.

    ​

Location & connectivity:

    Interactive map with nearby landmarks (schools, hospitals, IT hubs, highways).

    ​

Master layout & plot availability:

    Plot layout image.

    Table or grid of available plots with size, facing, price range, booking status.

    ​

Amenities & specifications:

    Roads, drainage, electricity, water, parks, clubhouse, security, etc.

Pricing & payment plans:

    Starting price per sq. yard / sq. ft, payment schedule, EMI or bank loan options (without copying bank details text).

    ​

Compliance & documentation:

    Brief list of approvals and key documents available for verification (no documents themselves).

CTAs and forms:

    “Book a Site Visit”, “Download Brochure”, “Talk to Our Advisor”, sticky call/WhatsApp button and short form.

        ​

    FAQs specific to the venture.

Services pages

For each service (Sell, Buy, Management, Construction, Resort):

    Clear service explanation:

        Who it is for, what problems it solves, and how you work step-by-step.

    ​

Process steps:

    Simple 3–6 step visual flow: enquiry, evaluation, documentation, transaction, post-sales support.

Benefits & USPs:

    For example: expert pricing assistance, legal verification support, end-to-end coordination.

Proof of credibility:

    Relevant testimonials, mini case studies, client logos, or project photos.

Lead capture:

    Tailored form (e.g., “List Your Property”, “Request Consultation”).

        ​

Listings / property search module

    Search & filter:

        Filters by property type (plot, villa, apartment), location, price range, size, status (available/sold), venture name.

    ​

Sorting:

    Newest, price low–high, price high–low, popularity.

List + map view:

    Cards for each property with a thumbnail, key stats, short description, and “View Details” CTA plus optional split view with map.

    ​

Property detail page:

    Images/video, description, location map, key features, price/offer details, downloadable brochure, enquiry form, similar properties block.

        ​

Important global components and modules

    Header:

        Logo, main navigation (Home, Ventures, Services, Listings, About, Blog, Contact).

        Prominent “Call Now” and “Book Site Visit” buttons.

        Language switch (if needed in future) and WhatsApp/chat icon.

    ​

Footer:

    Quick links to main pages, contact info, address, social media, legal links, newsletter signup.

    ​

Lead capture modules:

    Pop-up or slide-in “Get Project Details” form.

    Sticky bottom bar on mobile with Call / WhatsApp / Site Visit.

    Dedicated “Get a Callback in 15 Minutes” component.

    ​

Trust & social proof:

    Testimonials page.

    Video testimonials.

    Case studies (before–after value appreciation stories for plot investors).

    ​

Blog / resources:

    Articles about investing in plots, regional market trends, legal tips, loan guidance, NRI investment content.

    ​

Appointment & visit booking:

    Simple booking flow synced with a calendar tool to choose date/time for site visit or office meeting.

        ​

Additional modern features to consider

    Local SEO elements:

        Individual location pages (e.g., “Residential Plots in [Area/City]”), embedded Google Map, NAP consistency.

    ​

Landing pages for campaigns:

    Focused pages for each venture or offer, with minimal navigation, strong CTAs, social proof, and form.

    ​

WhatsApp and chat integration:

    One-click chat to ask price, send location, or share brochure.

Multi-device optimization:

    Mobile-first design, fast loading, compressed images, and minimal friction forms.

    ​

Analytics & tracking:

    Event tracking for CTAs, form submissions, WhatsApp clicks, and call clicks to measure campaign performance.

        ​

    NRI / investor-focused section (optional but powerful):

        Dedicated page explaining how NRIs can invest in plots, documentation needed, and remote booking support.

Potential missing elements you should add

    Dedicated “Our Ventures” hub highlighting the two active residential plot ventures prominently with badges like “Hot” or “Fast Selling”.

​

“Site Visit Experience” page explaining pickup, guided tours, safety, and what buyers can expect during visits.

“Customer Journey” or “How We Work” visual page for first-time buyers who are new to buying plots.

“Value Appreciation & ROI” section or page showcasing how previous ventures appreciated over time (without promising guaranteed returns).

“Download Center”:

    Brochures, sample agreements (redacted), checklists for plot purchase, FAQs in PDF.
